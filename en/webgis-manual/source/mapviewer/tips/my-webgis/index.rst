My WebGIS
===========

Under *My WebGIS*, tools are grouped together that let you adapt the operation and functionality
of WebGIS to your own needs.

With WebGIS, you can create your own (map) projects, define your own presentation variants, and
create bookmarks for geographic areas.

The options, differences, and recommended use cases are described here.
