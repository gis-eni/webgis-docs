Service Selection Lists
-----------------------

Some **map service servers** (e.g. **ArcGIS Server**) already offer built-in support for **domains**.
If these domains should be used, **manual parameterization** of the selection list can be **skipped**.

Instead, it is enough to simply set the **input field type** to ``domain``.
The **map viewer** recognizes the stored domains and fills the list **automatically** from the **service properties**.
